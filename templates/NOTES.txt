=== Option B: Per-Ingress Dynamic Certificates ===
Annotate any Ingress to get an automatic certificate:

  metadata:
    annotations:
      cert-manager.io/cluster-issuer: {{ include "cert-manager-bunny.issuerName" . }}
  spec:
    tls:
    - hosts:
      - your-app.web.us-west-1.{{ .Values.domain }}
      secretName: your-app-tls

Check certificate status:
  kubectl get certificate -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  kubectl get certificate --all-namespaces  # for per-ingress certs
